# Requirements Document

## Introduction

This specification focuses on creating a highly immersive and realistic laboratory environment for the Heart in a Jar simulation. The goal is to transform the current basic laboratory setting into a comprehensive, detailed cardiovascular research laboratory that enhances user engagement and provides an authentic scientific atmosphere. The environment should feel like a real medical research facility with proper equipment, realistic materials, interactive elements, and atmospheric details that support the educational and research objectives of the heart simulation.

## Requirements

### Requirement 1: Enhanced Laboratory Equipment and Instrumentation

**User Story:** As a researcher using the Heart in a Jar simulation, I want to see realistic medical and laboratory equipment around me, so that I feel immersed in an authentic cardiovascular research environment.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the laboratory loads THEN the system SHALL display professional medical equipment including ECG monitors, defibrillators, and cardiac monitoring devices
2. WHEN the user looks around the lab THEN the system SHALL show realistic laboratory instruments such as precision scales, pH meters, centrifuges, and microscopes with proper materials and textures
3. WHEN equipment is present THEN each device SHALL have appropriate LED indicators, displays, and control panels that appear functional
4. WHEN the laboratory is viewed THEN the system SHALL include proper medical storage cabinets, refrigeration units, and specimen storage areas
5. WHEN the environment loads THEN the system SHALL display realistic computer workstations with multiple monitors showing relevant medical data visualizations

### Requirement 2: Realistic Materials and Surface Details

**User Story:** As a user of the simulation, I want all surfaces and materials to look and feel realistic, so that the laboratory environment is convincing and immersive.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN surfaces are rendered THEN the system SHALL use physically-based materials with appropriate reflectance, roughness, and metallic properties
2. WHEN the user observes laboratory surfaces THEN the system SHALL display realistic wear patterns, scratches, and aging on equipment and furniture
3. WHEN materials are viewed THEN stainless steel surfaces SHALL show proper reflections and fingerprint marks
4. WHEN the laboratory is lit THEN glass surfaces SHALL demonstrate correct refraction, reflection, and transparency properties
5. WHEN floors are visible THEN the system SHALL show realistic laboratory flooring with proper tile patterns, grout lines, and subtle staining or wear
6. WHEN walls are observed THEN the system SHALL display appropriate laboratory wall finishes with realistic paint, tile work, and mounting hardware

### Requirement 3: Advanced Lighting and Atmospheric Effects

**User Story:** As a user experiencing the simulation, I want realistic lighting and atmospheric conditions, so that the laboratory feels like a real working research facility.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the laboratory is active THEN the system SHALL provide multiple lighting sources including overhead fluorescents, task lighting, and emergency lighting
2. WHEN lighting changes occur THEN the system SHALL demonstrate realistic light falloff, shadows, and color temperature variations
3. WHEN the environment is rendered THEN the system SHALL include subtle atmospheric effects such as dust particles, air circulation patterns, and humidity effects
4. WHEN different times of day are simulated THEN the system SHALL adjust natural lighting from windows with appropriate color temperature and intensity
5. WHEN equipment operates THEN the system SHALL show appropriate indicator lights, screen glows, and equipment-specific lighting effects
6. WHEN the laboratory is viewed THEN the system SHALL include realistic reflections on all appropriate surfaces including floors, equipment, and glass

### Requirement 4: Interactive Laboratory Elements

**User Story:** As a researcher in the simulation, I want to interact with various laboratory elements, so that I can feel engaged with the environment and understand how different systems work.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the user clicks on light switches THEN the system SHALL toggle appropriate lighting zones with realistic switching behavior
2. WHEN the user interacts with equipment THEN the system SHALL provide visual and audio feedback such as button presses, screen changes, and operational sounds
3. WHEN the user approaches information displays THEN the system SHALL show relevant laboratory information, safety notices, and equipment status
4. WHEN the user activates emergency systems THEN the system SHALL demonstrate proper emergency lighting, alarms, and safety protocols
5. WHEN the user interacts with storage areas THEN the system SHALL allow opening/closing of cabinets, drawers, and storage units with realistic animations
6. WHEN the user examines equipment THEN the system SHALL provide detailed information tooltips and operational status indicators

### Requirement 5: Environmental Audio and Ambient Effects

**User Story:** As a user of the simulation, I want to hear realistic laboratory sounds and ambient audio, so that the environment feels alive and authentic.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the laboratory is active THEN the system SHALL play appropriate ambient sounds including HVAC systems, equipment humming, and electrical buzzing
2. WHEN equipment operates THEN the system SHALL produce realistic operational sounds such as centrifuge spinning, monitor beeping, and pump cycling
3. WHEN the user interacts with elements THEN the system SHALL provide appropriate audio feedback including switch clicks, drawer slides, and button presses
4. WHEN emergency systems activate THEN the system SHALL play proper alarm sounds and emergency announcements
5. WHEN the environment changes THEN the system SHALL adjust ambient audio levels and characteristics based on lighting, equipment status, and user actions

### Requirement 6: Safety and Regulatory Compliance Visualization

**User Story:** As a researcher using the simulation, I want to see proper laboratory safety equipment and signage, so that I understand the importance of safety protocols in real laboratory environments.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the laboratory loads THEN the system SHALL display appropriate safety equipment including eyewash stations, emergency showers, fire extinguishers, and first aid stations
2. WHEN safety areas are visible THEN the system SHALL show proper safety signage, hazard warnings, and regulatory compliance notices
3. WHEN the user observes the laboratory THEN the system SHALL include appropriate personal protective equipment storage and disposal areas
4. WHEN emergency equipment is present THEN the system SHALL demonstrate proper placement, accessibility, and visual indicators for safety systems
5. WHEN the laboratory is viewed THEN the system SHALL show realistic chemical storage areas with proper labeling, ventilation, and containment systems

### Requirement 7: Dynamic Environmental Systems

**User Story:** As a user of the simulation, I want the laboratory environment to feel dynamic and responsive, so that it seems like a living, working research facility.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the simulation runs THEN the system SHALL demonstrate subtle environmental changes such as equipment cycling, indicator light patterns, and display updates
2. WHEN time passes THEN the system SHALL show realistic equipment warm-up sequences, standby modes, and operational cycles
3. WHEN the user is present THEN the system SHALL include motion-activated lighting, proximity sensors, and automatic equipment responses
4. WHEN environmental conditions change THEN the system SHALL adjust atmospheric effects, lighting intensity, and equipment behavior accordingly
5. WHEN the laboratory operates THEN the system SHALL demonstrate realistic power consumption indicators, equipment status changes, and system monitoring displays
